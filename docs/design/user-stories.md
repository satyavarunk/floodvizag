# FloodVizag User Stories

## Citizen

I want to know if my route is flooded before leaving home.

---

I want to report flooding in less than 30 seconds.

---

I want to receive updates about flooding near me.

---

I want to know whether a road is walkable, bikeable or closed.

---

## Emergency Services

I want to identify the worst flooded locations.

---

I want to know where flooding is increasing.

---

## City Officials

I want historical flood patterns.

---

I want to identify recurring flood hotspots.

---

I want data that helps improve future drainage planning.
